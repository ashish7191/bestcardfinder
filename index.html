Questions people ask

Does using bestcardfinder affect my credit score?

No. We never ask for your PAN, phone number or any detail that could trigger a credit enquiry. Everything runs in your browser.

How do you value reward points and miles?

Cashback is counted at face value. Points and miles are counted at a conservative redemption value, usually what you'd get on flights or vouchers through the bank's own portal, not best-case transfer partners. Open any card's breakdown to see the effective rate we used per category.

Why does a card with a ₹5,000 fee rank above a free one?

Because at your spend level it earns more than the fee costs. If the ranking flips at a lower spend, try trimming your numbers and you'll see it move.

Should I hold more than one card?

Often, yes. A common pattern is one card for your biggest category (for example Amazon or fuel) and a flat-rate card for everything else. Your results show the top earner in each of your spend categories.

How current is the card data?

Rates and fees are indicative as of November 2026 and banks change them often. Always confirm the latest terms on the issuer's website before you apply.
